## A Note from Ernest

Thank you for choosing to work with me. I know getting started with AI can feel overwhelming — there's a lot of noise out there, and most of it isn't designed for people with real jobs and real workflows. That's exactly why I do this work the way I do.

Think of our sessions like bringing in a knowledgeable neighbor rather than hiring a corporate consultant. We'll work at your pace, on your devices, and focus on what's actually useful to *you*. My job isn't to dazzle you with tech — it's to help you leave each session with something you can use tomorrow.

---

## What to Expect in Your First Session

Your first session is designed to be low-pressure and conversational. There's no quiz, no prerequisite knowledge, and no expectation that you already know anything about AI. We'll start by talking through how you work — what tools you use, what slows you down, and what you're hoping AI can help with. This isn't just small talk; it helps me tailor everything we do together to your actual situation.

From there, we'll do a short, hands-on demonstration using your own device. You'll interact with an AI tool directly — no watching me do it while you take notes. I'll walk alongside you, explain what's happening in plain terms, and answer questions as they come up. If something doesn't make sense, we slow down. If you want to go deeper on something, we go deeper.

By the end of the session, you'll have a clear picture of where you're starting from and where we're headed. Within 24 hours, I'll send you a written session recap covering what we covered, key takeaways, and a simple homework template to practice before our next meeting. You won't leave with a to-do list a mile long — just a few focused next steps that fit your schedule.

## Before Your First Session

Use this checklist to get the most out of our time together:

- [ ] **Bring your laptop or tablet** — the actual device you use for work, not a borrowed one
- [ ] **List 3 AI tools** you've heard of or are curious about (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot)
- [ ] **Write down 1–2 tasks** you do repeatedly that feel slow, tedious, or frustrating
- [ ] **Create a free account** at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) — I'll send setup instructions separately
- [ ] **Jot down your questions or concerns** — especially anything you're uncertain or skeptical about
- [ ] **Bring a notepad or open a notes app** — you'll want to capture a few things as we go
