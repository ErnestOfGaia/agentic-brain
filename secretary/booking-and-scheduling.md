# Booking and Scheduling — Ernest Of Gaia AI Coaching

I'm Ernest. You can book a free discovery call right here in the chat, or reach me directly by text
or email. Both work, and neither is the "proper" one.

## How to book a session

**In the chat.** Ask my scheduling assistant about availability and it will check my actual calendar
and show you what's genuinely open. Give it your name, your email and a time that suits you, it'll
read the booking back to you, and once you say yes it creates the event. A Google Calendar invite
arrives in your inbox. Nothing is booked until you confirm.

Auto-booking covers a set window of days and hours. The assistant will tell you the current window
when you ask, because it can shift with the season. If the time you want falls outside it, the
assistant won't book it and will point you at me instead, which is the right answer rather than a
limitation.

**By text.** Send "AI coaching" to **503-664-0546**. Texting beats calling, because the number
doesn't take voicemail, so a missed call leaves nothing behind for me to return.

**By email.** **eog@ernestofgaia.xyz**.

Text or email is also the way to reach me about anything that isn't scheduling, and about times
outside the auto-book window. I'll follow up within 48 hours of your first message.

## The free first call

Your first conversation is free: 30 minutes, online, no commitment and no card.

What you get out of it:

- Straight answers, including what AI can't do for you
- An honest read on whether coaching is the right fit. If it isn't, I'll say so
- One clear next step, whether or not you book anything

It's a conversation, not a sales call. Plenty of people book it just to ask questions.

## When sessions happen

Everything is by appointment. I don't hold open drop-in hours.

Coaching hours run on a set group of days and an evening-leaning window, in Pacific time. I'm not
listing the exact days and hours here on purpose: the scheduling assistant reads them live and will
tell you the current window, and a second copy written down somewhere would eventually disagree with
the calendar. Ask the assistant, or ask me.

If the time you want falls outside that window, text me. I can often make something work that the
automatic booking won't take on its own.

## Where sessions happen

In person, I come to you: your home or your workplace, working on your own devices rather than a
demo laptop. I cover Pacific City to Portland and the I-5 corridor.

Online sessions work anywhere. I'll send you a video link when we confirm the time.

If you're more than about an hour away, we'll do the first session online to see whether it's a good
fit before either of us commits to travel.

## Changing or cancelling a session

Reschedule any time. Twenty-four hours' notice helps me fill the slot, but there's no cancellation
fee and no deposit to lose.

If something falls through inside 24 hours, just tell me and we'll find another time. Life happens,
and I'd rather you reschedule than sit through a session you can't focus on.

## How often we meet

The plans are built around a weekly session, because a habit has to repeat on a short cycle before it
holds. We agree your rhythm together on the free call, and an interval you'll actually keep beats one
that looks better on paper.

You can book single sessions as you go with no commitment at all. If you'd rather lock in a longer
arrangement, there are three plans:

| Plan | Commitment | Savings |
|---|---|---|
| Pay as you go | None, book as you go | None |
| Habit | 3 months | None |
| Rhythm | 6 months | 5% off |
| Craft | 9 months | 10% off |

Start pay-as-you-go and move onto a plan whenever it makes sense. There's no penalty for staying
flexible.

## What happens in a session, and after

Sessions follow a simple shape: about ten minutes catching up on what worked since last time, forty
to sixty minutes of hands-on building, then ten minutes to recap and agree what you'll practise.

Within 24 hours afterwards I send you a written recap of what we covered, any custom prompts or
templates we built, and one focused exercise to try before we meet again.

We set the next date before we finish, so nothing drifts.

## What to have ready

For your first session, bring:

- The laptop or tablet you actually work on, not a borrowed one
- A free Claude.ai or ChatGPT account. I'll send setup instructions beforehand
- Three AI tools you've heard of or are curious about
- One or two tasks you repeat often that feel slow or tedious
- Anything you're uncertain or sceptical about
- Somewhere to take notes

You don't need to prepare beyond that, and you don't need to know anything about AI already.

## If you're booking for a team

Coaching sessions are one-on-one. If someone else on your team should be in the room, tell me when
we book and we'll plan the session around that.

Organisations booking a first session get a short set of questions from me beforehand, so I can
tailor the time to the tools your team already uses.

## Paying for sessions

The free call needs no card and no details.

We'll settle how you'd like to pay before your first paid session. Payment is per session unless
you've chosen one of the longer plans.

## Booking a nonprofit giveback session

For every 12 hours of paid coaching you complete, I donate one free session to a nonprofit you
choose. It doesn't have to be in Oregon.

Once you've earned one, tell me which organisation you'd like it to go to. I contact them directly
and schedule it, in person or online, and you're welcome to sit in.

If you don't have a nonprofit in mind, those hours go into my volunteer work with Team Rubicon,
where I've been a Grey Shirt for about a decade.
