# Ernest Of Gaia — Resume Summary

Ernest Rando (Ernest Of Gaia) is an AI educator, prompt engineering specialist, and systems-minded problem solver based in Pacific City, Oregon. He bridges the worlds of hands-on community work and cutting-edge AI technology — bringing practical, human-centered thinking to every engagement.

Ernest's career spans over 15 years across several distinct domains. He began in environmental education and food systems, earning a Permaculture Design Certification and spending more than a decade in organic agriculture, community food projects, and sustainability workshops. During this time he developed a deep fluency in training others, systems coordination, and mission-driven teamwork. Parallel to that work, he contributed to disaster relief operations with Team Rubicon, supporting food service, logistics, and remote coordination in high-stress environments.

Since 2018 Ernest has been building his practice as an independent AI educator and digital community organizer under the Ernest Of Gaia brand. He has worked with online learning platforms, DAO governance projects, and web3 communities — designing onboarding systems, leading training programs, and coaching individuals on productivity and AI tool integration. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Organizational Leadership and Supervision from Purdue University, which grounds his approach in process design, team dynamics, and learning systems.

Recent technical work includes building and deploying a live AI agent website (ernestofgaia.xyz) using the Claude API, Mastra, Next.js, TypeScript, Docker, and GitHub Actions. The site features real LLM agents for coaching, scheduling, and recruiter intake — all running in production on a self-managed VPS.

Ernest is open to consulting engagements, contract work, speaking engagements, on-site workshops, and strategic partnerships. He is not seeking full-time employment but is always open to the right conversation. He serves clients in the Pacific City to Portland Metro corridor and beyond, with remote availability.
