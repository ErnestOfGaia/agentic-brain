## 2. Core Offer

I provide in-person, one-on-one AI coaching and tutoring for individuals, small business owners, and non-technical professionals who want to understand and use AI tools in their real lives — without the hype, jargon, or sales pitch.

**Who I work with:** People who are not technical — and that's the point. Curious beginners, workers trying to keep up, small business owners overwhelmed by tech changes, anyone who wants step-by-step support using their own devices and their own workflow.

**What makes it different:** This isn't a course or a webinar. It's a relationship-based, long-term engagement — similar to hiring a private tutor or a trusted local tradesperson. We meet in person, use your actual devices, and focus on practical outcomes.

**Geographic service area:**
- Primary: Pacific City, Lincoln City, Tillamook

- In-person preferred; remote by arrangement
