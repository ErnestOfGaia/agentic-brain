# Ernest Of Gaia — Business DNA Record

*The brand record for Ernest Of Gaia AI Coaching: who it serves, what it offers, and how it presents itself across channels. The live site is canonical for services, pricing and plans.*

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**Business Name:** Ernest Of Gaia — Local A.I. Guide
**Aesthetic:** Local tradesperson meets local artist. Nature-based. Practical. Grounded. Not corporate, not hype-driven.
**Geographic Identity:** Pacific City, Oregon → Portland metro. Oregon Coast corridor. Eventually: Greater Western Sydney (Penrith), Australia.
**Tone of Voice:** Plain language. Honest. Practical. "Just the facts" quarterly review vibe. Warm but not salesy.

---

## 2. Core Offer

I provide in-person, one-on-one AI coaching and tutoring for individuals, small business owners, and non-technical professionals who want to understand and use AI tools in their real lives — without the hype, jargon, or sales pitch.

**Who I work with:** People who are not technical — and that's the point. Curious beginners, workers trying to keep up, small business owners overwhelmed by tech changes, anyone who wants step-by-step support using their own devices and their own workflow.

**What makes it different:** This isn't a course or a webinar. It's a relationship-based, long-term engagement — similar to hiring a private tutor or a trusted local tradesperson. We meet in person, use your actual devices, and focus on practical outcomes.

**Geographic service area:**
- Primary: Pacific City, Lincoln City, Tillamook
- Extended: Portland metro area
- In-person preferred; remote by arrangement

---

## 3. Target Market

**Primary audience:** Non-technical adults (individuals and small business owners) in the Oregon Coast–Portland corridor

**Demographics:**
- Occupation types: Local trades, small retail, hospitality, administrative, professional services
- Tech comfort level: Low to moderate — comfortable with phones and basic apps but not coding or AI tools
- Discovery channels: Local business card displays (RV dealers, automotive, artisan shops, tour guides), word of mouth, local Google search

**Secondary audience (future):** Western Sydney / Penrith, Australia — same demographics, similar market conditions

## 6. Marketing Approach

**Secondary channels:** Local presence (business cards at local businesses, van signage), Google Business Profile (local SEO)

## 7. Risk Literacy & Ethics Stance

A core part of the offering. Clients learn not just how to use AI tools but:
- What AI can't do (and why that matters)
- Common risks and failure modes
- How to stay safe and avoid costly mistakes
- How to evaluate AI output critically

This distinguishes the service from "AI hype" content and builds long-term trust with non-technical clients.
