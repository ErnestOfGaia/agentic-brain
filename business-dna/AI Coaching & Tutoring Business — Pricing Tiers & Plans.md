# AI Coaching & Tutoring Business — Pricing Tiers & Plans

> **Business model:** 1-on-1 personalized AI coaching sessions, structured across three tiers. Oregon-market pricing calibrated to Astoria/Coast, Salem, and Portland demographics. For every 12 hours of paid coaching completed, one complimentary session is donated to a local nonprofit of the client's choice.

## Tier 1 — Foundations (AI Starter)

**Rate:** $75/session · 60 minutes · Best fit: Astoria / Oregon Coast market

### Who this is for
Beginners with little to no AI experience. Solopreneurs, small retail/hospitality owners, educators, and anyone who's heard of ChatGPT but hasn't made it useful yet.

### What's included per session
- ChatGPT and Claude basics — what they are, what they're not
- Prompt anatomy: role, context, task, format, constraints
- AI safety, hallucinations, and fact-checking habits
- Setting up Claude.ai (free vs. Pro) and navigating the interface
- Introduction to Claude Projects for organizing client work
- Session recap document delivered within 24 hours
- Homework prompt templates to practice between sessions

### Tools covered
- Claude.ai (Free & Pro tiers)
- ChatGPT (Free & Plus)
- Google Gemini (intro)
- Notion AI (optional intro)

## Nonprofit Giveback Program

> For every **12 hours of paid coaching** completed, the coach donates **1 complimentary session** to a local Oregon nonprofit of the client's choice.

### How it works
1. Client completes 12 sessions (any tier)
2. Client nominates a local Oregon nonprofit
3. Coach contacts the nonprofit to schedule a free session
4. Session covers: AI basics, one practical workflow, and a resource list
5. Optional: client co-attends as a mentor (great for their own learning)

### Eligible organization types
- 501(c)(3) nonprofits in the client's county or region
- Oregon Food Bank chapters
- Schools and school districts (Salem-Keizer, Astoria, Portland Public)
- Community action agencies (Clatsop Community Action, etc.)
- Libraries, co-ops, arts organizations, environmental groups
- Tribal organizations and Indigenous-led nonprofits

### Tracking
- Log paid session count in client's Claude Project notes
- At session 12, note is flagged: "🎁 Nonprofit session available"
- Track donated sessions publicly (website counter or newsletter) for community trust-building

## Session Workflow (Standard Operating Procedure)

```
Before session:
  □ Review client's Claude Project — last session notes, homework
  □ Pull up their prompt library doc
  □ Prepare 1–2 new techniques or tools to introduce

During session (60–90 min):
  □ Check-in: what worked from homework? (10 min)
  □ Build or refine: hands-on work in Claude/ChatGPT (40–60 min)
  □ Wrap-up: recap what was built, assign homework (10 min)

After session (within 24 hours):
  □ Update Claude Project with session notes
  □ Send client: recap doc, updated prompt library, homework
  □ Log hours toward nonprofit threshold
  □ Schedule next session
```
